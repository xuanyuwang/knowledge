## Problems and Lessons

- A label lookup revision is not historical provenance. Encoding it into stored coaching identifiers would have broken revision-independent targets and made legacy/new rows semantically inconsistent.
- Profile-level feature enablement does not guarantee use-case enablement; rollout validation must compare explicit scope sets rather than trust inheritance or PR titles.
- A successful beta path does not prove the official frontend release consumes the same code/config. Release-channel comparisons are required before calling a customer fix complete.
- Training Simulator rules must be filtered before Opera execution. UI-only applicability would leave runtime annotations and picker eligibility incorrect.
- Reporting design must include never-started assignments, zero-run tasks, normalized conversation/quiz outcomes, authorization, and pagination limits; a task-run-only happy path would produce biased metrics.
- Repository-wide validation wrappers may fail for environmental reasons even when owning scoped validators pass; record the limitation and do not weaken credential or dependency safeguards to force an aggregate command.
