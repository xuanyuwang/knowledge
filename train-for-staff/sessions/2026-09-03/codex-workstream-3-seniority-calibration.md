## Findings

- Strong Senior behavior is consistently demonstrated in ambiguous problem ownership, consequential technical judgment, cross-system diagnosis, risk-managed rollout, and production validation.
- Staff-like behavior recurs within bounded technical domains through systemic reframing, explicit semantic contracts, architecture for future work, and evidence-led influence.
- The evidence does not yet establish broad Staff scope because multi-team alignment, execution through others, adopted leverage, roadmap ownership, and measurable business outcomes are sparse or unmeasured.
- Artifact creation is evidence of judgment and intent; it is not proof of organizational leverage until others adopt it or it changes team outcomes.
- The most accurate current shorthand is “established, high-performing Senior Engineer with bounded Staff-like behavior.”
