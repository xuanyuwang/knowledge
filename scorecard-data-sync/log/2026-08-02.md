# 2026-08-02

- Confirmed the root cause of the regional scorecard and conversation gaps: unbatched Distributed inserts generated approximately 6.36 million small spool files while the 16-thread sender pool was saturated across 3,328 Distributed tables.
- [PR 312915](https://github.com/cresta/flux-deployments/pull/312915) enabled batched sends and split-on-failure; [PR 312923](https://github.com/cresta/flux-deployments/pull/312923) raised the sender pool from 16 to 48 and rolled the Conversations cluster.
- Guitar Center data became visible at 01:09 EDT; all reported customers caught up and the incident was resolved by 01:17.
- Verified at 11:59 EDT that the regional queue had fallen to 224 files / 2.5 MB and Guitar Center/HCD's conversation, score, and scorecard queues were empty.
- Published the full timeline, root-cause analysis, rejected hypotheses, and follow-ups in `deliverables/2026-08-us-east-distributed-backlog-incident.md`.
