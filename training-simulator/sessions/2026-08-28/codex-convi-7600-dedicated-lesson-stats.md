## Review feedback disposition

### Adopted

- Reuse `TrainingSimulatorModuleStats` rather than a lesson-only module rate
  message.
- Preserve exact `TrainingLesson.training_modules` order and require stable
  aligned lesson-response order.
- Keep `BUILD.bazel` Gazelle-owned and verify it by running Gazelle.

### Adapted

- The old filter-placement concern is resolved by restoring
  `ListTrainingLessons`; the dedicated request owns only `time_range` and no
  agent/group/team filters.
- The optional average concern is resolved with a normal scalar plus
  `applicable_score_count`, which distinguishes unavailable from a real zero
  without a generated oneof.
- Pass reporting uses passed/total assignment counts rather than a rate scalar,
  and score availability uses the explicit count denominator.
- The old missing-result warning is removed under the accepted
  failure-equivalent overall zero/false/N/A decision.

### Not retained

- The old `include_stats`, parallel list-response suppression, and reporting
  fields on `ListTrainingLessons` were specific to the superseded transport.
- The old user filter and the earlier group/team ideas have no current product
  or authorization evidence on this reporting surface.
- The inherited module message has no `behavior_name`; module name plus
  `criterion_id` remains its accepted criterion identity.

The sole remaining lesson-branch API-lint suppression is
`core::0131::synonyms=disabled`, matching the adjacent custom Retrieve statistics
RPCs. It is narrowly necessary because `Retrieve` is the established custom
reporting verb rather than the linter's preferred standard verb.
