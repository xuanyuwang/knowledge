## Findings

- Three distinct positions fit the evidence: cross-system data correctness and reliability, product infrastructure and data-platform building, and domain-oriented product systems engineering.
- Correctness and reliability has the strongest completed proof but risks narrowing the profile to reactive debugging or SRE-adjacent work.
- Product infrastructure best matches the documented growth direction toward reusable team capability, but organization-wide adoption is still emerging rather than proven.
- Domain-oriented product systems work captures semantic and product judgment, but cross-functional influence and the desirability of stakeholder-heavy work require direct validation.
- The repository supports growth preferences around reusable capability and customer/business connection, but it does not establish which daily activity mix is most energizing.

## Decision

Use product infrastructure as the first forward-looking hypothesis to test, anchored by the stronger correctness/reliability evidence. Preserve the other two as real alternatives until role-market feedback and explicit personal-preference evidence justify narrowing them.
