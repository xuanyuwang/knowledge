## Problems and Lessons

- A technically valid first implementation direction can still be the wrong long-term API boundary when responsibility, discoverability, and failure isolation are weighed explicitly.
- Production remediation is safer when backup publication, checksum verification, source/projection cleanup, and UI read-back are treated as one workflow instead of independent tasks.
- Permission-debugging work needs to separate true authorization failures from older UI-state gates, or the follow-up fix will target the wrong layer.
- Historical reporting semantics often self-correct at query time, but the labels shown alongside those results may still reflect a different timestamp or lifecycle concept and need precise customer wording.
