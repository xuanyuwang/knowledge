# INSI-4251: Missing data in Performance Insights

## Objective and impact

Restore PostgreSQL-to-ClickHouse scorecard projection for Guitar Center and Home Care Delivered and verify Performance Insights completeness. Missing QM data affects agent performance and month-end incentive reporting.

## Current status

Resolved on 2026-08-02. The root cause was a region-wide ClickHouse Distributed sender backlog: the 16-thread background scheduling pool was saturated across 3,328 Distributed tables while unbatched inserts generated millions of small files. Batching, split-on-failure, a pool increase to 48, and the associated rolling restart drained the queue and restored customer data.

At approximately 20:40 UTC, HCD's original cohort improved to 3,860/11,954 missing (32.29%), but its newer cohort remained 810/1,924 missing (42.10%). Guitar Center remained 100% missing: 49,652 rows in the original cohort and 26,116 since 10:00 UTC. Guitar Center's queued score/scorecard data grew to approximately 373 MB.

At 20:57 UTC, a five-minute Guitar Center smoke repair used the monitor's normal `ReindexScorecardsWorkflow` path for 124 targeted conversation scorecards. Temporal completed successfully, but ClickHouse still showed 0/124 visible because the writes joined the existing Distributed backlog. Do not expand the backfill until delivery resumes.

At 22:40 UTC, the production monitor's exact ID comparison for the full Pacific-local July 31 day found 49,509/49,686 missing (99.64%), 93 stale, and 84 fully synced. This supersedes earlier ad hoc time-field counts that reported zero visible for broader cohorts; those queries did not perform the monitor's PG-ID-to-CH-ID comparison.

At peak, `us-east-1-prod` held approximately 6.36 million files / 107.6 GB across 61 databases. By 11:59 EDT on August 2, only 224 files / 2.5 MB remained region-wide, and Guitar Center and HCD had zero queued conversation, score, or scorecard files.

## Findings

- Backfill job success does not mean ClickHouse delivery or Performance Insights visibility completed.
- PR 30530 and CustomerSnapshot conversion errors are unrelated to score mapping.
- Guitar Center re-score cleanup uses `ALTER TABLE ... ON CLUSTER DELETE`; a shared DDL task has remained incomplete on all nine hosts since July 30.
- Customer-specific Distributed queues still hold `scorecard_d` and `score_d` data.

## Next actions

1. Add queue file-count, byte, and oldest-age alerts.
2. Add sender-pool saturation monitoring and replace the suppressed `BgDistSchPool` signal.
3. Evaluate applying batching and split-on-failure defaults to other regions.
4. Make job/monitor observability distinguish spool acceptance from shard-local visibility.
5. Add queue-aware monitor semantics and a Distributed-backlog runbook.
