## Findings

- The shared conversation establishes that the file is intended as governing guidance for career discovery, with career positioning first and resume writing second.
- The proposed direction was compatible with the repository, but the document treated evidence inventory, synthesis, seniority calibration, positioning, and resume construction as future sequential phases.
- The foundational inventory and synthesis already exist across project narratives, resume bullets, published writing, weekly summaries, the mid-year review, and the scorecard-sync interview story.
- `CAREER_POSITION.md` is most useful as an operating brief that governs how those artifacts are built and refined, rather than as the final career assessment itself.
- Detailed project evidence should remain in its canonical product domain and be promoted into `train-for-staff`; it should not be duplicated into this document.

## Changes

- Replaced the sequential phase model with status-aware, ongoing workstreams.
- Preserved the evidence-first instructions, seniority calibration method, iterative career-hypothesis loop, target-role analysis, resume rules, and missing-evidence discipline from the source conversation.
- Mapped each career need to its canonical artifact and aligned evidence collection with the repository's promotion path.
- Updated `project.yaml` so the new strategy document is discoverable and the objective reflects the current work.

## Follow-up

- Build two or more interview stories that complement the existing database-consistency narrative.
- Recover adoption, team-leverage, cross-team influence, and business-impact evidence where available.
- Revisit the positioning only when new evidence changes the recurring pattern or target-role criteria.
