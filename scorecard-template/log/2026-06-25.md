## Key Finding

AutoQA-created scorecards bypass the coaching `CreateScorecard` API. Live AutoQA and backfill both write through shared scoring persistence after `MapToScores(...)` and `ComputeScores(...)`. Therefore, a rule such as "scorecards must have at least one score" needs to be enforced in those paths or in the shared persistence helper.

- Added `deliverables/empty-scorecards-workflow-and-api-analysis.md`.

- Mapped empty-scorecard behavior across evaluation, AutoQA, appeal, group calibration, and analytics.
- Captured API-level behavior for create/update/submit, AutoQA live/backfill, ClickHouse projection, QA score stats, QA conversations, appeal stats, and group calibration stats.
- Key finding: Postgres persistence can allow empty scorecards, while ClickHouse and analytics APIs are mostly score-row driven and therefore filter or skip them.
