## Findings

- The right next role adds problem-framing authority, end-to-end production ownership, identifiable internal users for reusable capability, and execution through others—not merely a harder queue of implementation work.
- Product Infrastructure, data-intensive Backend, and application-facing Data Platform roles are the strongest title families; Distributed Systems, Product Backend, and Staff roles need mandate-specific screening.
- The current general resume leans toward correctness/reliability and contains a user-filter completion claim that conflicts with the latest canonical project record.
- A product-infrastructure projection is stronger when it leads with external tables, scorecard consistency, fleet monitoring and repair, bounded user-filter standardization, and Group Calibration breadth.
- The interview portfolio has one fully ready story, several strong briefs, and a clear gap around mentoring, delegation, measured adoption, and named cross-functional influence.
